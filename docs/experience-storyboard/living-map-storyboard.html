03 · Component design

Build the map from clear, independent pieces.

Map scene

Owns the landscape, responsive viewport and safe placement of layers. A meaningful still image is the fallback.

Ambient layers

Separate water, foliage, lantern and lighting layers. Motion is subtle, paused offscreen and optional.

Destination controls

Labeled accessible hotspots on the map and an equivalent Places list share the same destinations.

Destination panel

Shows a heading, content, static characters, Back and Close. On mobile it becomes a sheet or full-screen panel.

Story content

Interest, timeline, project and contact components own their respective content and states; no fabricated facts.

Clock and appearance

Clock determines the map's ambient phase. A separate user-selected light/dark interface theme controls readability.

04 · Interaction and accessibility

Every destination works without animation.

Open, explore, return

  • A hotspot or Places button opens the same panel. Closing restores the previous map position and invoking control.
  • Nested details have Back; Escape closes the active layer. Browser Back and direct links need useful destinations.
  • Keyboard, touch and screen-reader visitors can navigate a normal labeled list without precise map gestures.
  • Mobile keeps labels legible and offers a list; it does not shrink the full map into tiny targets.

Fallback and recovery

  • Missing map or animation assets never hide the Places list or content.
  • Reduced motion removes ambient movement and uses immediate changes; reading remains unaffected.
  • Loading, missing media and form errors show recovery; entered form data is preserved on failure.
  • Clock changes never block navigation, obscure labels or make the panel unreadable.

The first production proof needs a small layered map area, one correctly positioned hotspot, a panel with static character artwork and a gradual lighting transition. Test keyboard, touch, reduced motion, loading and frame timing on desktop and a representative phone before expanding it.

Open decisions and artwork: the visual day and night treatments, mobile map composition, exact destination illustrations and time-zone policy remain design proposals.
